Rock Hammering

High-powered hydraulic rock breaking for tight spaces, restricted zones, and projects where blasting isn't an option.

Service Overview

No Permit. No Problem. Just Results.

Not every rock problem can be solved with a blast. In urban areas, near sensitive structures, or in confined spaces, hydraulic rock hammering is the right tool for the job. Our powerful breaker attachments deliver controlled, high-energy impacts to break up solid rock without the use of explosives.

Rock hammering is ideal for smaller volumes of rock, isolated boulders, confined excavations, and any location where blasting permits or safety constraints make explosives impractical. We bring the same expertise and heavy equipment capability to hammering jobs that we bring to every project.

Service Capabilities

Precision hydraulic breaking for rock removal in any location and any conditions.

No Explosives Required

All rock breaking is achieved through mechanical force. No blast permits, no magazine storage, no waiting — just fast mobilization and effective results.

Works in Restricted Areas

Safe for use near buildings, utilities, and populated areas where blasting regulations or safety zones would otherwise prevent rock removal.

Precise Material Removal

Hydraulic hammers can be directed with precision, targeting specific areas of rock while leaving surrounding material undisturbed.

Confined Space Capability

We operate equipment in tight access situations such as building excavations, utility trenches, and areas with restricted overhead clearance.

Minimal Ground Vibration

Compared to blasting, hydraulic hammering produces significantly lower ground vibrations — protecting adjacent structures and sensitive infrastructure.

Boulder & Oversize Breaking

Large boulders and oversize rock fragments from prior blasting operations are reduced to manageable sizes for loading and hauling.

How It Works

Our Process

01

Site Assessment

We evaluate the rock volume, access constraints, nearby structures, and project requirements to determine the most efficient approach.

02

Equipment Selection

The right hammer size and excavator combination is chosen for the job, balancing breaking power with the access and footprint restrictions on site.

03

Systematic Breaking

Rock is broken systematically, working from the edges inward or top-down depending on site conditions to maximize efficiency and safety.

04

Material Removal

Broken rock is loaded and removed from the work zone. The area is graded and cleaned up, and the site is ready for the next phase of work.
