Drilling & Blasting

Engineered precision. Controlled power. From site surveys to post-blast assessment, we handle every phase of the operation.

Service Overview

Precision Drilling. Controlled Results.

Our drilling and blasting services are built around engineering-driven blast design and decades of field experience. Every job starts with a thorough site assessment and ends with a clean, fragmented result that meets your project's exact specifications.

Whether you're working on a highway cut, a building foundation, or a mining excavation, we engineer the blast for your specific rock type, depth, and fragmentation requirements — keeping surrounding structures and personnel protected at every step.

What's Included

Service Capabilities

A complete, end-to-end drilling and blasting service — from initial assessment through post-blast cleanup.

Precision Drill Pattern Design

Custom drilling patterns optimized for rock type, density, and fragmentation goals. Every hole is placed for maximum efficiency and minimum waste.

Engineered Blast Design

Charge weights, timing sequences, and detonation methods engineered for your project's geology and constraints. No guesswork — just results.

Vibration & Air Blast Monitoring

Continuous seismograph monitoring protects adjacent structures and ensures compliance with regulatory vibration limits throughout every blast.

Fly Rock & Dust Control

Protective blast mats, exclusion zones, and dust suppression measures are standard on every job to contain the blast and protect the surrounding area.

Post-Blast Assessment

After every blast, our team assesses fragmentation results and documents outcomes, ensuring targets were met and the site is safe to re-enter.

Regulatory Compliance

We handle all required permits, notifications, and documentation. Full compliance with state and federal blasting regulations on every project.

How It Works

Our Process

01

Site Assessment

We evaluate the geology, rock hardness, structure proximity, and project requirements to form the foundation of the blast plan.

02

Blast Engineering

Our team designs the drill pattern, charge loads, and timing delays to achieve the specified fragmentation while controlling vibration and flyrock.

03

Precision Drilling

Drill rigs execute the approved pattern with accuracy. Hole depths and angles are verified before loading begins.

04

Controlled Blast & Cleanup

The blast is executed under controlled conditions. Post-blast inspection is completed and the site is cleared and ready for the next phase of your project.
