Rock Excavation

Systematic, efficient removal of rock material to create the space your project demands — on schedule and on spec.

Service Overview

Move Rock. Create Opportunity.

Rock excavation is the backbone of major construction projects. Where soil ends and solid rock begins, Capps Blasting takes over. We deliver clean, efficient excavation that prepares your site for whatever comes next.

Our approach combines precision blasting with heavy excavation equipment to break, load, and remove rock material efficiently. We leave your site graded to spec and ready for foundation work, paving, or the next phase of construction.

How It Works

Our Process

01

Geotechnical Survey

We assess the site geology, rock type, and project specifications to develop an effective excavation strategy.

02

Excavation Planning

A detailed excavation plan is developed that accounts for blast design, equipment access, material removal routes, and grade targets.

03

Rock Breaking & Blasting

Controlled blasting or hydraulic breaking is used to fragment the rock, followed immediately by excavator loading.

04

Removal & Grade Finish

Material is loaded and removed from site. Final grading is completed to the specified elevations and the site is documented and handed over.
