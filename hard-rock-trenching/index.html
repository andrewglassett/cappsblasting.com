Hard Rock Trenching

Precision trench cutting through the toughest ground — engineered to run straight, clean, and on depth.

Service Overview

When the Ground Pushes Back, We Push Harder.

Trenching in solid rock is one of the most challenging tasks in site work. Standard excavation equipment can't handle it. Capps Blasting can. We combine controlled blasting and hydraulic hammering to cut precise, clean trenches through any rock formation.

Our hard rock trenching service supports utility contractors, municipalities, and developers who need trenches cut to specified widths and depths for water mains, sewer lines, gas lines, conduit runs, and drainage systems.

How It Works

Our Process

01

Route Survey & Layout

The trench alignment is surveyed, staked, and checked for underground utilities before any work begins.

02

Surface Preparation

Overburden soil and loose material are stripped from the work zone and the rock face is exposed and assessed.

03

Rock Breaking & Excavation

Controlled blasting or hydraulic hammering is used to break the rock along the trench alignment, followed by excavator cleanup.

04

Cleanup & Handover

Rock spoil is removed, the trench floor is graded to grade, and the trench is handed over to the utility installation crew.
