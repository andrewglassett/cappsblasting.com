Earth Moving & Site Development

From raw land to a project-ready site — we handle the heavy work so your build can begin.

Service Overview

The Ground Beneath Every Great Build

Every major construction project starts with site development. Before foundations are poured or structures rise, the land must be cleared, graded, and prepared. Capps Blasting delivers the heavy earthwork that sets your project up for success.

From small residential lots to large commercial or industrial developments, we bring the right equipment and expertise to get your site properly prepared — on time and to the required specifications.

What's Included

Service Capabilities

Comprehensive site development services from land clearing through final compaction.

Mass Grading

Large-scale earthwork to bring your site to the designed grade elevations, establishing the platform for all subsequent construction.

Cut & Fill Operations

Efficient movement of material from high areas to low areas, balancing earthwork quantities to minimize import and export costs.

Land Clearing & Stripping

Removal of vegetation, topsoil, and surface material to expose the subgrade and prepare for grading operations.

Drainage & Stormwater

Site grading designed to direct surface water appropriately, including swales, berms, and drainage channel construction.

Subgrade Preparation

Preparation of subgrade materials to achieve the bearing capacity required for pavements, slabs, and structures.

Compaction & Testing

Lift-by-lift compaction of fill materials to specified densities, with field testing to document and certify results.

How It Works

Our Process

01

Site Evaluation

We review site plans, walk the property, and assess existing conditions including topography, soil, and rock content.

02

Land Clearing

Vegetation and topsoil are removed and stripped from the work area. Material is either stockpiled for reuse or hauled off-site.

03

Cut & Fill Earthwork

Rock and soil are moved, blasted as needed, and graded to bring the site to the designed elevations per the civil engineering plans.

04

Final Grade & Compaction

Subgrade is finished to final tolerances and compacted to specification. Site is documented and turned over ready for the next trade.
