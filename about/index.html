About Capps Blasting

A family-owned company built on hard work, precision, and an unwavering commitment to safety for over two decades.

Our Story

Forged in the Field

Capps Blasting was founded with a simple principle: do the job right, keep everyone safe, and stand behind your work. What started as a small drilling operation has grown into one of the region's most trusted names in blasting and site development.

Our team of certified blasters, drillers, and equipment operators brings a combined wealth of experience to every project. We work alongside general contractors, developers, municipalities, and private landowners — delivering the kind of results that come from genuine expertise.

  • Family-owned and operated
  • Fully licensed and insured in all operations
  • Certified explosive handlers on every job
  • Modern fleet of drilling and excavation equipment
  • Available for both large commercial and smaller private projects

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These aren't words on a wall. They're the principles that guide every decision we make on and off the job site.

01

Safety First, Always

Every crew member goes home safe. We enforce strict safety protocols, conduct regular training, and never cut corners when it comes to protecting people and property.

02

Technical Excellence

Blasting is both a science and a craft. We invest in ongoing training, up-to-date equipment, and engineering-based blast design to ensure optimal outcomes every time.

03

Straight Talk

You get honest assessments, realistic timelines, and transparent pricing. No runarounds. We tell you exactly what the job involves and what it'll take to get it done.

04

Accountability

We stand behind our work. If something isn't right, we make it right. Our reputation was built one job at a time, and we protect it on every project.

Capabilities

Built to Handle Any Job

Our modern fleet of drilling rigs, excavators, and support equipment allows us to mobilize quickly and handle projects of varying scale. Whether it's a tight residential excavation or a large commercial site clearing, we have the tools and experience to deliver.

  • Hydraulic rock hammers and breakers
  • GPS-guided equipment for precision work
  • Vibration and air blast monitoring systems
  • Full fleet of excavators, loaders, and haul trucks
